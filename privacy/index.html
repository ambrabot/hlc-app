Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026 · Applies to the HLC Club app (web, Android and iOS) and app.healthyfoodrecipesclub.com.

Wellness & Healthy LifeStyle Club ("HLC Club", "we", "us") builds a functional-nutrition companion app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address (and optional first name)To create your account and sign you in with a one-time code. This is the only personal identifier we require.
Wellness check-in answers (energy, sleep, focus, digestion, inflammation) and your goalsTo personalize recipe recommendations and show your progress over time. Stored against your account.
Saved favorites and in-app activityTo sync your saved recipes and remember your preferences across devices.
Purchase records (membership / programs)To unlock the content you bought. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store your full card details.
Photos you scan (packaged products or plates)Processed only to identify the item and estimate nutrition. Product barcodes are looked up via Open Food Facts. Plate photos may be sent to our processing provider (Cloudflare Workers AI) to estimate foods and macros; they are used only to return your result and are not used to build an advertising profile.
Basic usage & device data (approximate, via analytics)To understand how the app is used and fix problems (see "Analytics" below).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

How your data is handled

  • Account data is stored in our database (Cloudflare D1) and served through our secure API over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in uses a short-lived one-time code sent to your email — we do not store passwords.
  • Some preferences (e.g. your chosen language, recent scans) are stored locally on your device.

Service providers

We share the minimum necessary with providers that help us run the app, each under their own privacy terms:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Brevo — sending you account and (if you opt in) wellness emails.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, database, and on-request photo/nutrition processing (Workers AI).
  • Open Food Facts — public product database used for barcode lookups.
  • Microsoft Clarity — privacy-friendly product analytics (usage patterns, not identity).

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate usage and improve the app. It may capture interaction data (taps, page views, device type) to produce heatmaps and diagnostics. It is not used to identify you personally or to advertise to you.

Your choices & rights

  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, at any time.
  • You can unsubscribe from wellness emails via the link in any email; account emails (like sign-in codes) are essential to the service.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or erase your data.
To exercise any of these rights, email info@healthyfoodrecipesclub.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: open Account → "Delete my account & data" and confirm. This immediately removes your profile, wellness check-in answers, saved favorites and membership record from our systems, and cancels any active subscription so you are not billed again.

Prefer not to use the app? Email info@healthyfoodrecipesclub.com from your account address and we will delete your data for you.

Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data except where we must keep limited records (e.g. purchase/tax records) for legal reasons.

Children

HLC Club is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Health disclaimer

HLC Club provides educational nutrition and wellness content. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not replace your healthcare provider.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
