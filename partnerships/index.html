Why the moment matters

Health-intent is worth most at the point of decision.

Brand awareness is cheap; awareness at the second someone chooses what to eat is not. HLC sits inside three high-intent moments — and each one is a natural place for the right partner to appear.

Scanning

In the aisle

A shopper scans a packaged product. Our Clean Check reads the ingredients and processing, then offers the honest, cleaner path forward — a moment of trust and a moment of consideration.

Planning

At the meal plan

Someone builds their week around a goal — anti-inflammatory, gut, hormone balance. Every recipe resolves to a concrete ingredient list: demand, specified.

Buying

At the cart

Ingredients become a basket. This is where a brand placement, a retailer, or a delivery platform turns intent into a completed purchase.

The intelligence offer

Trend intelligence — aggregated, anonymized, privacy-first.

HLC is a first-party, consented view of what health-intent shoppers actually want. We package that into aggregate, anonymized trend intelligence — the demand signals a category leader can't easily get anywhere else. Privacy-first isn't a limitation here; it's the reason the signal is trustworthy and the reason a health-sensitive audience keeps using us.

What we share

  • Aggregated category trends — e.g. rising healthy-dessert ingredients, clean-swap demand by category
  • First-party, consented, cohort-level signal — never traceable to a person
  • Purchase-intent direction: what shoppers are moving toward and away from
  • Co-developed insight to shape products, placement and seasonal timing

What we never sell

  • Personal or individual user data — ever
  • Individual behavioral profiles or PII
  • Anything that could identify a single member
  • Health-sensitive data outside explicit consent and purpose limits (LGPD / CCPA by design)
