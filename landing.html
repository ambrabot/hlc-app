Good questions

Before you start

Do I need an account to try the scanner?

No. Open the app and scan — the Clean Check verdict is free, with no login and no wall. You only create an account when you want to save favorites or join the Club.

Is HLC medical or nutrition advice?

No. HLC is an educational companion built on a functional-nutrition perspective. It helps you understand food and supports your goals — it never diagnoses, treats, or replaces your doctor or a registered dietitian.

What makes the desserts "healthy"?

Every recipe is built gluten, dairy & refined-sugar free, using whole-food ingredients — with the macros and the functional "why" shown for each. They're designed to satisfy the craving without the crash.

Can I cancel the Club anytime?

Yes. It's month-to-month (or yearly if you want two months free), cancel in a tap, no questions. Most people join only after the free scanner and recipes have already earned it.
